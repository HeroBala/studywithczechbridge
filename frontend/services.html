Key Insights for the China Admissions & X1 Visa Procedure

Essential regulatory guidelines for international applicants seeking degrees in China.

1. Form JW201 vs JW202

Issued by the Ministry of Education of China. Form JW201 is allocated for CSC Chinese Government Scholarship recipients. Form JW202 is for self-funded students. Both are required for obtaining your X1 Visa.

2. Foreigner Physical Examination

Must be completed at an official hospital using the standard China physical exam form with hospital stamp, ECG, blood test report, and chest X-ray. Valid for 6 months from the date of issue.

3. CVASC X1 Visa & 30-Day Window

Long-term study visa (X1) allows entry into China with a 30-day validity window. Within 30 days of arrival, our coordinators guide you to convert the X1 visa into a multi-year PSB Foreigner Residence Permit.

4. CSC & Provincial Scholarships

Full CSC Scholarships cover 100% tuition, free university accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, and a monthly stipend (¥2,500/mo Bachelor, ¥3,000/mo Master, ¥3,500/mo PhD).
