Good to Know

Intakes & Requirements

🗓️

Intakes

Main intake in September; several universities also offer a February intake. Applications typically open 6–10 months ahead.

🇬🇧

English Requirement

Most programs accept IELTS 6.0–6.5 or equivalent. Some universities offer their own English tests — we'll advise what fits you.

📜

Academic Documents

SSC & HSC certificates (or bachelor's degree for master's programs), transcripts, and a valid passport. We handle the nostrification guidance.
