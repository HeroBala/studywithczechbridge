The Application Process

Seven clear steps from your home in Bangladesh to your university in the Czech Republic.

Free Consultation

Create your free account or contact us. We review your academic background, English level, budget and goals, then give you an honest picture of your options. Timeline: 1–3 days.

University & Program Selection

We shortlist 2–4 universities and programs that best fit your profile, comparing tuition, city, admission requirements and deadlines together with you. Timeline: about 1 week.

Document Preparation & Upload

You upload your passport, SSC/HSC certificates, transcripts, photos and IELTS results to your online dashboard. We check every file and guide translations and notarization where required. Timeline: 1–3 weeks.

Application Submission

We prepare and submit your applications, pay attention to every deadline and requirement, and set your online status so you always know where things stand. Timeline: varies by university.

Admission & Offer Letter

Once the university accepts you, you receive your official acceptance letter — the key document for your visa application. Timeline: 2–8 weeks after applying.

Visa Processing

We assemble your complete long-term student visa file — accommodation proof, financial documents, insurance — and prepare you for the embassy interview in Dhaka. Timeline: 60–90 days.

Travel to the Czech Republic

Visa in hand, you book your flight with our guidance. We coordinate your arrival, housing check-in and first-week orientation in your new city. Welcome to Europe! 🎉
